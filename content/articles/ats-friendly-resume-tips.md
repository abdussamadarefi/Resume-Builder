# 10 Tips to Make Your Resume ATS-Friendly

## Introduction
Learn how applicant tracking systems work and what you can do to ensure your resume passes automated screening.

## Key Strategies for Success
1. **Focus on Measurable Outcomes**: Highlight numerical metrics like percentage increases, revenue gains, or time saved.
2. **Tailor Keywords**: Match your technical and functional keywords with the exact job specification.
3. **Keep Formatting Clean**: Use standard semantic structures and consistent margin spacing.
